Questions

What is a fly tying hook chart?

A fly tying hook chart lists hooks by manufacturer and model alongside their specifications — size range, wire strength, shank length, gape and eye type — so tyers can pick the right hook for a pattern. Traditional charts are static PDFs covering a single brand; the Woolly Bug Hook Finder is a searchable database covering 700+ hooks from 16 manufacturers.

What is a hook comparison chart?

A hook comparison chart (or cross-reference chart) maps equivalent hooks across manufacturers — for example, which Ahrex or Fulling Mill hook matches a given Tiemco model. Hook Finder does this instantly by comparing real specifications rather than relying on out-of-date printed tables.

How do I find equivalent fly tying hooks?

Search for the hook you know in the Woolly Bug app — by brand and model — and Hook Finder shows comparable hooks from other manufacturers matched on shank length, wire strength, gape and eye type.

Can I compare Tiemco to Ahrex hooks?

Yes. Both Tiemco and Ahrex are in the database, along with 14 other manufacturers, so you can cross-reference models between any of them.

Can I find barbless alternatives?

Yes. Every hook in the database records whether it is barbed or barbless, so you can filter for barbless equivalents of any hook — ideal for competition anglers and catch-and-release fisheries.

Which manufacturers are included?

Ahrex, Dai-Riki, Daiichi, Demmon, Fario, Fasna, Firehole, Fulling Mill, Gamakatsu, Hanák, Hends, Kamasan, Mustad, Partridge, Sprite and Tiemco — with more added in updates.

Can I use Hook Finder without the app?

No. Hook Finder lives in the Woolly Bug app, where you can search all 706 hooks, filter by specification and see equivalents across brands. Reference pages for individual models are published on woollybug.com/hooks/ for looking something up, but comparing and finding equivalents is done in the app.
