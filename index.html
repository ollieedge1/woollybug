Everything a fly tyer needs

From your first hook to your hundredth pattern, Woolly Bug keeps your whole bench organised and at your fingertips.

Material inventory

Track every feather, dubbing, hook and thread with photos, quantities and storage locations. Clone items and batch-add — your whole bench in one searchable library.

Hook Finder

Search 650+ hooks from 14 brands by shank, gape, wire weight and eye type — and find equivalent alternatives in seconds.

Shopping basket

Build shopping lists linked to your inventory. Mark items purchased and restock — so you never buy a duplicate spool again.

Multi-device sync

Cloud sync plus WiFi-direct between iPhone and iPad — your stash stays in step at the bench or the shop, even without internet.

CSV import & export

Bulk-manage your inventory in Excel or Numbers, with smart de-duplication when you re-import. Your data, always yours.

Pattern keeper

Save your favourite fly recipes with full material lists, then see at a glance which patterns you can tie right now and which materials you're missing.
