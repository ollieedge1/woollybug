Questions

Is Woolly Bug free to download?

Yes. It's free on the App Store, with an upgrade when you need more room.

Do I need an internet connection to use Woolly Bug?

No. Everything works offline and syncs to the cloud when you're back online.

What kinds of fly tying materials can I track?

Everything on your bench: hooks, thread, dubbing, feathers, beads, wire, flash and more, with sizes, colours and quantities.

Can Woolly Bug tell me what I can tie right now?

Yes. Save your patterns and Woolly Bug shows which you can tie and what's missing.

Which devices does Woolly Bug support?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, kept in sync.
