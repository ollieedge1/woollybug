How many Partridge hook models are there?

The Woolly Bug database lists 92 Partridge models, covering 21 applications. Of these, 33 are barbless and 59 are barbed.

Which Partridge hooks are most commonly used?

Commonly referenced Partridge models include Partridge 15BN, Partridge 15BNX, Partridge ACS, Partridge ACS/E, Partridge ACS/X, Partridge ACSS. Each links through to full specifications and equivalent hooks from other manufacturers.

What sizes do Partridge hooks come in?

Size runs vary by model. Each Partridge model page lists its own size range, and the full range with filtering is in the free Woolly Bug app.
