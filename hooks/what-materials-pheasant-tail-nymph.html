What materials do you need to tie a Pheasant Tail Nymph?

7 components · full recipe

To tie a Pheasant Tail Nymph you need 7 materials: hook, thread, tail, rib / weight, body, thorax, bead. Frank Sawyer's Pheasant Tail Nymph is a masterclass in doing more with less — originally just pheasant tail fibres and copper wire, no thread at all. It imitates baetis nymphs and remains one of the most reliable subsurface patterns ever devised.

Full material list

ComponentWhat to use
HookStandard or 1XL nymph hook, size 14–20
ThreadBrown or none (Sawyer used wire only)
TailPheasant tail fibres, 3–4
Rib / WeightFine copper wire
BodyPheasant tail fibres, wound
ThoraxPheasant tail fibres or peacock herl
BeadCopper or tungsten (optional)

What each material does

Hook. Sawyer's original used a standard shank; modern versions often use a curved or jig hook.

Thread. The traditional dressing uses fine copper wire as both thread and rib.

Tail. Short — around half a shank length.

Rib / Weight. Does three jobs: ribs the body, adds weight, and reinforces the fibres.

Body. The natural mottling is what makes this pattern work; no dubbing needed.

Thorax. Slightly built up to suggest the emerging wing case.

Bead. A beadhead version fishes deeper and suits euro nymphing rigs.

Which hook suits a Pheasant Tail Nymph?

A Pheasant Tail Nymph is tied on a nymphs hook. The Woolly Bug database lists 100 hooks suited to this style — see best hooks for nymphs for the full list and what the specifications mean.

Which colours should you tie?

Natural pheasant tail is the classic. Dyed black or olive fibres give useful variants for different water.

Frequently asked questions

What materials do you need to tie a Pheasant Tail Nymph?

A Pheasant Tail Nymph needs 7 components: Hook (Standard or 1XL nymph hook, size 14–20); Thread (Brown or none (Sawyer used wire only)); Tail (Pheasant tail fibres, 3–4); Rib / Weight (Fine copper wire); Body (Pheasant tail fibres, wound); Thorax (Pheasant tail fibres or peacock herl); Bead (Copper or tungsten (optional)).

What hook do you use for a Pheasant Tail Nymph?

Standard or 1XL nymph hook, size 14–20. Sawyer's original used a standard shank; modern versions often use a curved or jig hook.

What size Pheasant Tail Nymph should you tie?

size 14–20 covers most situations. Smaller sizes for clear water and selective fish, larger for coloured water.

Can you substitute materials in a Pheasant Tail Nymph?

Yes — most components have workable substitutes, and colour is the most flexible variable. The one thing worth matching properly is the hook style, because it determines the finished fly's proportions.
