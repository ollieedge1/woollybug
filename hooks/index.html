Frequently asked questions

What is a fly tying hook database?

A fly tying hook database catalogues hooks by manufacturer and model alongside their specifications — shank length, wire strength, bend, eye type, size range and whether they are barbed or barbless. This one covers 16 manufacturers and lets you find equivalent hooks across brands.

How do I find an equivalent fly tying hook?

Search the hook you know in the Woolly Bug app and open its equivalents list. Matches are calculated from real specifications — gape and shank length matter most, because they determine the finished fly's proportions, followed by wire strength, which changes how the fly sits in the water.

Which hook manufacturers are covered?

Ahrex, Dai-Riki, Daiichi, Demmon, Fario, Fasna, Firehole, Fulling Mill, Gamakatsu, Hanak, Hends, Kamasan, Mustad, Partridge, Sprite Hooks, TMC.

What do 1XL, 2XH and similar hook codes mean?

Shank codes describe length relative to standard: 1XL is one size longer, 1XS one size shorter. Wire codes describe gauge: XH is heavier wire (sinks, stronger), XF is finer wire (lighter, better flotation for dry flies). The app explains what each hook's own codes mean in practice.

Is the hook database free?

Yes. The full searchable database of 700+ hooks, with filtering, cross-brand equivalents and inventory tracking, is in the free Woolly Bug iPhone and iPad app.
