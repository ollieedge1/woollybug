Best hooks for beadhead nymphs

87 hooks from 16 manufacturers

The Woolly Bug database lists 87 hooks suited to beadhead nymphs, across 16 manufacturers. The most common specification in this category is a Std. shank with 1XH wire, and 48 of the 87 are barbless. Beadhead hooks need a gape and eye that let a bead seat cleanly at the head. Heavier wire helps the fly turn over and sink, and a wide gape leaves clearance once the bead and dubbed thorax are in place.

What specifications matter for beadhead nymphs?

Shank length. Most hooks in this category use a standard shank, which matches the proportions of most classic dry fly and nymph recipes — if a pattern doesn't specify a shank length, this is what it assumes.

Wire strength. The typical choice here is heavy wire, one step stronger than standard, adding a little sink rate and useful margin when fishing for larger fish.

Barbed or barbless. 48 of the 87 hooks listed for beadhead nymphs are barbless from the factory. Competition rules and many catch-and-release fisheries require barbless, and a barbed hook can be pinched down with flat-jawed pliers before dressing.
