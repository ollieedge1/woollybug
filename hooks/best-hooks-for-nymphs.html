Best hooks for nymphs

100 hooks from 16 manufacturers

The Woolly Bug database lists 100 hooks suited to nymphs, across 16 manufacturers. The most common specification in this category is a Std. shank with Std. wire, and 52 of the 100 are barbless. Nymph hooks trade flotation for sink rate. Heavier wire gets the fly down in the column, and a slightly longer or curved shank gives room for a dubbed abdomen and thorax. Many tyers pair these with a tungsten bead at the head.

What specifications matter for nymphs?

Shank length. Most hooks in this category use a standard shank, which matches the proportions of most classic dry fly and nymph recipes — if a pattern doesn't specify a shank length, this is what it assumes.

Wire strength. The typical choice here is standard wire — a balance of strength and weight that works across most freshwater applications.

Barbed or barbless. 52 of the 100 hooks listed for nymphs are barbless from the factory. Competition rules and many catch-and-release fisheries require barbless, and a barbed hook can be pinched down with flat-jawed pliers before dressing.
